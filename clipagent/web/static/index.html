clipagent is an open-source AI clipping agent. It finds the moments worth sharing, follows whoever is talking, adds captions and scores every clip, all on your own machine.

Questions

Is clipagent free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT licence and runs on your own computer or server. If you add an AI key, you pay Anthropic or Google directly for the few requests it makes per video.

Does it work without an API key?

Yes. Without a key, a built-in scorer picks clips by looking at hooks, pacing, payoff phrases and audio energy. A Claude or Gemini key makes the picks noticeably better and adds titles, descriptions and hashtags.

Which videos can I use?

Any video or audio file ffmpeg can read (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, MP3 and more). Links from YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch and X work when you run clipagent yourself.

Do my videos leave my computer?

No. Transcription, face tracking and rendering all happen locally. If you set an AI key, only the transcript text is sent to Claude or Gemini to choose highlights.

How does it decide who to follow in the frame?

It tracks every face, measures how much each mouth moves while there is speech, and points a smoothed virtual camera at whoever is talking. When two people take turns, it can switch to split-screen.
