# clipagent studio

Open-source AI clipping agent: upload a long video and get vertical clips with
captions, speaker tracking and a virality score.

- Studio: `/app` · Website: https://clipagent-ten.vercel.app
- Source: https://github.com/nagadattasaikondoju-ship-it/cliping-agent-

This public studio limits video length and size, processes one video per
visitor at a time, and deletes projects after 24 hours.
